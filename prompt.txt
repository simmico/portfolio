Build a stunning single-page portfolio website for graphic designer Sirshen Moodley. This must be production-grade, fully mobile responsive, and hosted as a single index.html with all CSS and JS embedded. any words should not have em dashes, ai slop and must be UK English

FONTS: Cormorant Garamond (serif, headings) and Montserrat (sans, body) from Google Fonts.

COLOURS:
- Background: #1e1e1e
- Gold accent: #B8965A
- Light text: #e8e0d4
- Muted text: #9a9080
- Surface: #252525

---

HERO SECTION (full viewport height):
- Subtle animated grain texture overlay on the background (CSS noise or SVG filter)
- His name "SIRSHEN MOODLEY" assembles letter by letter on page load, each letter drops or fades in with a staggered delay, slow and cinematic, about 80ms between each letter
- Below the name, "Graphic Designer" fades in after the name finishes, letter spacing animates from wide to normal
- A single thin gold horizontal line draws across the screen left to right after the title appears
- A slow subtle parallax background -- dark abstract geometric shapes or lines drift very slightly as the user scrolls
- Scroll indicator arrow at the bottom pulses gently

---

WORK SECTION (the centrepiece):
This is the most important part. Make it extraordinary.

Layout: Full-width alternating -- odd pieces align left with text right, even pieces align right with text left. On mobile, stack vertically. Each piece takes up 85vw, generous spacing between them, almost magazine editorial pacing.

SCROLL ANIMATIONS -- this is critical, make each one feel different:
- Every image starts hidden. As it enters the viewport via IntersectionObserver, a gold-coloured overlay panel sweeps across the image from left to right (CSS clip-path animation) then disappears to reveal the image underneath -- slow, cinematic, about 800ms total
- The alternating pieces come from opposite directions -- odd items reveal left to right, even items reveal right to left
- After the image reveals, the client name and category text slides up from below with a slight delay
- As the user scrolls past each piece, apply a very subtle parallax to the image itself -- it moves slightly slower than the scroll speed, creating depth
- On desktop hover: a dark semi-transparent overlay slides up from the bottom of the image (not a fade, a physical slide), revealing the project description in Cormorant Garamond italic. The image itself scales up very slightly (1.03) with a slow ease.

CUSTOM CURSOR (desktop only):
- Replace the default cursor with a small gold dot (8px, #B8965A, border-radius 50%)
- It follows the mouse with a slight lag (lerp interpolation, smoothing factor 0.12) for a trailing feel
- On hover over any project image, the dot expands smoothly to 40px and the border becomes a thin gold ring (outline, not fill)
- On hover over links and buttons, the dot morphs to a small horizontal line
